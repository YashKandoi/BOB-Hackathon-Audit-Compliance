KYC Rules: 
#### Current Account
**Requirement:** Proof of Identity  
**Description:** Submit a valid Aadhaar number, PAN, or any other Officially Valid Document (OVD).  
**Citation:** RBI Guidelines 2024, Section 16【4:4†source】

**Requirement:** Proof of Address  
**Description:** Submit a recent utility bill, rent agreement, or any other OVD containing the address.  
**Citation:** RBI Guidelines 2024, Section 16【4:4†source】

**Requirement:** PAN Verification  
**Description:** PAN must be verified from the issuing authority's verification facility.  
**Citation:** RBI Guidelines 2024, Section 16【4:4†source】

#### NRI Accounts
**Requirement:** Proof of Identity  
**Description:** Submit a valid passport, visa, and immigration endorsement.  
**Citation:** RBI Guidelines 2024, Section 44【8:8†source】

**Requirement:** Proof of Address  
**Description:** Submit proof of address in the home country and a local address in India within 30 days.  
**Citation:** RBI Guidelines 2024, Section 44【8:8†source】

**Requirement:** PAN Verification  
**Description:** PAN must be verified from the issuing authority's verification facility.
